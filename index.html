WINDOWN kept since you start

WINDOWN · a diary

A full-screen place to write, closed with a password, kept in one encrypted file.

what it is
You write. You commit a line and it settles into the record. You can add to a past day, and the day shows that you came back to it.

what it isn't
No accounts. No cloud. No streaks, counts, or reminders. Nothing leaves your machine.

Once a line is committed you can't edit it. That's the point.

Download for Windows

A single .exe. Nothing to install. Windows 10 / 11.

The password can't be recovered. If you forget it, the diary is gone. This is by design. There is no reset and no back door.

The file is the diary. It lives beside the app as one encrypted file. Keep a copy somewhere safe; if you delete it, it's gone.
